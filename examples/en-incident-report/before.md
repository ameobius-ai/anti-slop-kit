# Incident post-mortem

In the interest of full transparency, we wanted to take a moment to delve into the events of last Tuesday, when a subset of our valued customers may have experienced degraded performance. At approximately 14:00 UTC, our monitoring systems began to surface a number of anomalous signals which were subsequently investigated by our world-class engineering team. It was ultimately determined that the root cause was related to an unexpected interaction between a recently deployed change and our robust caching layer. The issue has since been fully remediated, and we have implemented a comprehensive set of safeguards in order to ensure that similar incidents are unlikely to recur going forward. We deeply apologise for any inconvenience this may have caused and we remain committed to delivering the reliable experience our users rightly expect from us.
